# High-detail direct reconstruction

## Method and limits

Prefer suitable genuine supplied vectors. Otherwise author semantic paths/text from reference landmarks. No Image Trace, VTracer, automatic pixel contours or external vectorization by default. Do not describe automatic tracing as direct drawing. An explicitly requested automated route is a separately agreed method.

High fidelity is the aim, not a pixel-identity guarantee. More nodes, sharpening and upscaling cannot recover missing/compressed/occluded details. Ask for better source material only when a significant detail cannot otherwise be resolved.

## Separate content

1. Text: verify content and rebuild live text with measured baseline, width, font and weight; check symbols.
2. Linework: author bonds, curves, dashed frames, arrows and inhibitory bars separately; preserve connectivity and endpoints.
3. Subjects: draw irregular contours, internal boundaries and recognition-critical morphology; avoid generic substitutes.
4. Tone: native gradients/opacity in Illustrator plus separate vector detail; a smooth gradient does not replace fibres. Portable conversion has distinct limits.

## Diagnose detail loss

Inspect source-sized exports and app display. Distinguish fractional-zoom antialiasing from missing paths, smoothing, clipping, wrong font, lost contrast and quantization. Preserve naturally soft shading.

For a complex region, test its drawing approach with thin strokes, small text, irregular boundaries and tone before scaling it up. Compare at full size and 200%, correct and recheck. Use local overlays/edge differences for uncertain alignment. QA rasters stay outside delivered vectors.

## Practical acceptance

Track material differences in count, local curves, overlap, continuity, baselines, endpoints and tone. Regional work shares original coordinates; eliminate duplicate boundaries and seams.

Working drafts may be reviewed in the app before stylistic refinement is complete. State concrete scientific/technical issues when present. On explicit user approval, record visual acceptance and stop unrequested polishing instead of a generic failure footer. Never turn approval into a false technical-test or save claim.

If repeated safe attempts do not improve a concrete issue, explain the limit and options; do not loop indefinitely or restart/close user documents.
